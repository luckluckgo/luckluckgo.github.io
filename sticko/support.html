Sticko — Support

Getting started (Mac)

  1. Sticko lives in your menu bar. Click its icon and choose New Note, or press ⌘N.
  2. Drag a note anywhere on your desktop and resize it from its edges.
  3. Use the note's title bar to change its color, pin it above other windows or adjust its transparency.
  4. Open the Note List (⌘⇧L) to search, filter by tag, export or print.

iPhone and iPad

Install Sticko on your iPhone or iPad and sign in to the same Apple Account. Your notes appear automatically. Add the Sticko widget from your Home Screen's edit mode to keep a note in view.

Sync

Sticko syncs through iCloud Drive. Make sure iCloud Drive is turned on in System Settings (Mac) or Settings (iPhone/iPad) → your name → iCloud → iCloud Drive, and that every device uses the same Apple Account.

Troubleshooting

  • Notes don't appear on another device: check that iCloud Drive is on and that the device is online. The first sync after installing can take a minute.
  • "Maximum notes reached": raise the limit in Settings → Max Notes on your Mac, or delete notes you no longer need.
  • Widget shows an old note: open the Sticko app once so it can refresh the widget.
