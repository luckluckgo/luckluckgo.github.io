Sticko — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

This policy applies to Sticko for macOS and Sticko for iPhone and iPad, including the Sticko widget (together, "the Apps").

Data collection

The Apps do not collect, store or transmit any personal data to the developer or to any third party. There are no accounts and no sign-in.

iCloud sync

Your notes are stored on your device and synced through your own iCloud Drive. Sync is handled entirely by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The developer has no access to your notes.

Data stored on your device

  • Your notes, kept in a local database and in your iCloud Drive.
  • Preferences such as default note color, font size and maximum note count.
  • On iPhone and iPad, a small copy of your note text shared with the Sticko widget so it can display your notes. It never leaves your device.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The Apps contain no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and collect no usage data.

Children's privacy

The Apps do not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
