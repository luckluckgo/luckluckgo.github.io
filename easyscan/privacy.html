Privacy Policy — EasyScan

Last updated: 2026-04-16

EasyScan ("the app") is a document scanner that runs entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

What we do not collect

  • No account registration.
  • No analytics or crash reporting.
  • No server-side storage of your scans.

Advertising

The free tier of EasyScan displays banner advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. We have configured AdMob to serve non-personalized ads (NPA), so the app does not collect or share your IDFA, and you will not see the App Tracking Transparency prompt. AdMob may receive coarse, non-identifying signals (such as your country and device type) needed to deliver an ad. Pro users do not see ads.

For details, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Camera access

The app requests camera access solely to capture documents for scanning. Images are processed on-device using Apple's Vision framework. Nothing is uploaded.

Local storage

Scanned documents are saved to the app's private Documents folder on your device. You can delete them at any time from within the app. Deleting the app removes all scans.

In-App Purchases

EasyScan offers an optional one-time "Pro Upgrade" purchase that unlocks unlimited scans. Payments are processed by Apple via StoreKit; we do not receive or store any payment information.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
