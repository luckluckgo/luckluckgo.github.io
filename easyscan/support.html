EasyScan — Support

Getting started

  1. Open the app and tap the camera button.
  2. Point your iPhone at a document. The app detects the edges automatically.
  3. Tap the shutter, or enable Auto to capture hands-free.
  4. Adjust the corners if needed, then enhance and save.

Multi-page scanning

Tap "Multi" on the camera screen to start a multi-page session. Add pages one by one, then export as a single PDF.

Troubleshooting

  • Camera access denied: Open Settings → EasyScan → enable Camera.
  • Document not detected: Place it on a contrasting background with even lighting. Use the torch in low light.
  • Blurry scans: Hold the phone steady; the warning banner tells you when the image is sharp enough.

Pro Upgrade

EasyScan is free with a limited number of scans. When you run out, watch a short ad to get more scans, or buy the one-time Pro Upgrade for unlimited scanning with no ads. Restore purchases from the upgrade screen.
