fidelplayer — Support

Hi-Res audiophile music player for iPhone

fidelplayer plays your local library — DSD (DSF/DFF), FLAC, ALAC, WAV, AIFF, MP3, AAC and more — through a real, high-precision DSP engine: parametric and linear-phase EQ, AutoEQ, convolution, up/oversampling and bit-perfect output.

Getting started

Import music with the Import button (Files app — choose files or a whole folder). Tap a track to play. Use the EQ and Settings tabs to shape the sound.

Frequently asked

Where are my files? Imported audio is copied into the app's storage and is visible in the Files app under fidelplayer. Deleting the app removes them, so keep your originals or use Export Library in Settings to back them up.
