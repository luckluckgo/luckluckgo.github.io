fidelplayer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-04

fidelplayer collects no personal data. It is a fully on-device audio player. Your music files, settings, and listening activity never leave your iPhone, and the app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, or network communication.

Data we collect

None. fidelplayer does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Your music and files

Audio files you import are copied into the app's private storage on your device and are used solely to play and process them locally. They are accessible to you through the Files app and are removed if you delete the app.

Settings and on-device storage

Preferences (equalizer, audio settings, trial date, and per-track gain values) are stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage. They are never uploaded.

Purchases

The optional one-time "fidelplayer Pro" unlock is processed by Apple through the App Store. fidelplayer does not receive or store your payment information; purchase validation is handled by Apple's StoreKit on your device.

Tracking

fidelplayer does not track you and does not use the App Tracking Transparency framework, because it accesses no tracking data.

Children's privacy

fidelplayer does not collect any data from anyone, including children.
